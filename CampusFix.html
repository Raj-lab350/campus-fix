Welcome to CampusFix

Report. Track. Resolve.

CampusFix is a simple complaint management system for our college. Students can report problems like broken lights, water issues or dirty classrooms, and the admin can update the status until the problem is fixed.

Submit Complaint Track Complaint

How it works

Step 1: Submit

Fill the complaint form with your details, the category and the location of the problem.

Step 2: Get your ID

After submitting you get a unique complaint ID. Save it so you can check your complaint later.

Step 3: Track

Enter your complaint ID on the Track page to see if it is Pending, In Progress or Resolved.

Step 4: Resolved

The admin looks at the complaint, updates the status, and marks it Resolved when it is fixed.
